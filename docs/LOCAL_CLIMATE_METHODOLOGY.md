# Local climate methodology

The supplied calendar says Zone 8a while both chart images say 8b. These statements are retained
as source evidence and are not silently reconciled.

Canadian Plant Hardiness Zones and USDA extreme-minimum zones use different definitions and
must not be treated as interchangeable.

April 15 and October 15 remain source-reported frost anchors, not Errington climate normals.
No automatic replacement is made without a station-specific or authoritative climate record
that identifies geographic applicability and probability.

The existing Nanaimo A station is a candidate regional proxy only. Errington microclimate,
coastal exposure, elevation, cold-air drainage, and station distance must be considered.
